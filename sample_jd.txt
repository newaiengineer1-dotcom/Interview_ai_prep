SYNTHETIC JOB DESCRIPTION — LENDER TECHNICAL ADVISOR, SOLAR + BESS

Role:
Lender Technical Advisor — Renewable Energy / Solar + BESS

Responsibilities:
- Perform technical due diligence for utility-scale solar PV and BESS projects.
- Review energy yield assessments and key assumptions.
- Assess technology selection, warranties and degradation assumptions.
- Review grid connection studies and compliance requirements.
- Assess EPC, O&M and major technical contracts from a lender perspective.
- Identify material technical risks and proposed mitigations.
- Prepare concise technical reports for lenders and investors.
- Challenge project assumptions using available technical evidence.

Requirements:
- Engineering degree or equivalent technical background.
- Experience in utility-scale renewable energy.
- Knowledge of Solar PV and BESS.
- Strong technical report writing and stakeholder communication.
- Ability to identify technical risks and explain them clearly.
- Experience with due diligence is preferred.
